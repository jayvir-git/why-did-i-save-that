What was actually analyzed?

Group names describe inspected representatives, not every member. A save does not establish agreement, intent or belief. “Needs context” includes mixed groups, unassigned posts, similarity below 0.35, a margin below 0.025 between the top two groups, or no usable extracted text. These are review heuristics, not calibrated confidence.

Search checks all words across captured post text, quoted text and complete extracted attachment text, with complete-phrase matches first. Grouping uses bounded excerpts. OCR and agent interpretations remain separate. Image variants can appear in evidence, but are deduplicated for grouping. Full videos have not been watched or transcribed.
